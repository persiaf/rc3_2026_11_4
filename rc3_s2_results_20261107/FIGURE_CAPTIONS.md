# Figure captions and interpretation

All figures are generated from saved CSVs, with one panel per fixed seed in the order 11622026, 11622027, 11622028. A missing phase is labelled “no completed updates”; no endpoint is synthesized. All curves stop at actual logged completed updates. Line smoothing uses a trailing mean of up to 16 recorded update means and introduces no model evaluation. Vector PDF and 300 dpi PNG versions use the same data. Axis labels carry units; titles are kept in these captions.

1. **`fig01_rl_return` — Observed complete RL returns.** Thin translucent lines show each completed update's arithmetic mean across its 4 instances × 3 starts. Solid lines show the trailing16 mean of those update means. The return is the recorded complete `−J_H`; larger is less cost on the observed draw. Parent tasks alternate with different local sizes and obstacle/endpoint conditions, so the curve is descriptive, not a paired test of learned improvement.

2. **`fig02_rl_objective` — Mixed REINFORCE objective diagnostics.** Thin lines show the recorded signed batch objective; solid lines show a trailing16 mean. Leave-one-out baselines, sampled log probabilities, mixture weights and changing inputs determine the scale and sign. A lower loss does not establish better planning, and this figure must not be compared numerically with continued-SL CE.

3. **`fig03_raw_and_H_costs` — Observed costs before and after frozen H.** Columns correspond to seeds; rows show objective `J=L+0.75K`, route length `L`, and stop count `K`. Dashed orange and solid blue curves are trailing16 means of the same observed trajectories before and after H. Raw-to-H differences quantify the frozen postprocessor's contribution on recorded routes, not independent neural-policy gain. Across updates, tasks change and comparisons are unpaired. Exact terminal metrics are preferred; any fallback algebra is labelled in the trajectory CSV.

4. **`fig04_gradient_clipping` — Recorded optimization diagnostics by phase.** Upper and lower rows show RL and continued-SL separately. Orange solid and blue dashed traces show the global parameter-gradient norm before and after clipping; the horizontal reference is the approved norm cap1. Values use a logarithmic axis; exact zero, if present, is drawn at 10⁻¹⁴ only for display. Nonzero gradients and successful clipping indicate update mechanics, not planning-quality improvement.

5. **`fig05_advantage_and_entropy` — RL signal distributions.** Upper panels show the per-update median and 5th–95th percentile band of all12 detached LOO advantages; the band is an observed within-update distribution, not a confidence interval. Middle panels show the exact-zero advantage proportion. Lower panels show sampled-decision entropy in nats (forced first choices have no scored entropy), with a trailing16 mean and translucent raw update means. Summary files retain the complete min/p05/p25/median/p75/p95/max and zero/all-zero diagnostics.

6. **`fig06_continued_SL_CE` — Continued supervised optimization on the fixed teacher pool.** Thin traces show the recorded mean masked teacher CE over each batch's route-mean losses; solid traces show trailing16 means. This phase replays the already saved teacher labels and makes no new teacher/H queries. CE has a different objective/scale from REINFORCE and is not a comparative planning-performance metric.

7. **`fig07_wall_and_terminal_components` — Recorded cumulative wall time and terminal components.** Upper panels use the shared S2 clock, including admitted preparation, navigation, H, validation, checkpoint and other charged work up to each logged update; continued-SL starts at the fixed phase offset512. The dotted horizontal line is the 120min cap. These phase curves are parts of one clock and must not be added. Lower panels show trailing16 mean H, export and validation time per recorded RL trajectory; cache-hit trajectories have zero incremental component times. Event and ledger counts, pending/failed work, fixed development checks and final I/O remain available separately; these curves are not exhaustive resource ledgers.

8. **`fig08_observed_cost_by_window` — Observed training cost distributions by window size.** Points are arithmetic trajectory means, with vertical interquartile segments (p25–p75), for raw and H objectives in each actually observed window group. Segments are descriptive distribution ranges, not confidence intervals, and the three starts of one instance are dependent. Obstacle mode, endpoint condition and their combinations are exported separately in `group_summary.csv`. This figure contains training draws only and does not compare generalization or fixed endpoint policy quality.

9. **`fig09_frozen_H_gain` — Frozen postprocessor's observed contribution.** Thin traces show the per-update arithmetic mean of paired within-route differences `J_raw−J_H`; solid traces show trailing16 means. The difference belongs to the fixed H postprocessor on the recorded proposals. Its variation across updates also reflects changing tasks and proposals; it is not an estimate of independent network learning gain.

Development checks at the preregistered 256/512 cursors are used only for feasibility/resource reporting. No plot uses development objective values to select or rank weights. Frozen SL, native baselines, S3 and original60 performance are not evaluated here.
